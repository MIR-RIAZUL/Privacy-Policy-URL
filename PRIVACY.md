# Privacy Policy — SnapVault

**Last Updated:** November 2026

SnapVault is a 100% offline Windows desktop backup utility.

- **No Data Collection:** SnapVault does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, files, usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry.
- **100% Local Operation:** All backups, file catalogs, and settings remain strictly on your PC and the external USB drives or local network folders you choose.
- **No Internet Required:** SnapVault makes zero outbound network requests and requires no user account.
